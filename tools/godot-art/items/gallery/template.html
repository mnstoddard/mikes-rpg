Models are glTF (.glb), Y-up, in metres, following the shared Emberfall art style guide. One model covers every metal and wood: in Godot, ItemLook.apply_game_item(model, "sword_steel_yew") swaps the materials named metal, wood, bark, jewel_metal, gem and leather for the shared 512 px texture library, just like the swatches here. The textures inside each file are light preview versions.
