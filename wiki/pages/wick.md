**Wick** was one of the two highest members of [[The Ember Circle]], beside [[Archmage Vale]]. Every child in Embervale knows his name as the worst in history. The Emberguard call him Wick the Betrayer, or the Snuffed One.

## What everyone is taught

- On the night of the great working, Wick struck down [[King Aldemar XVIII]] and seized the working for himself. That was [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]].
- He hid his crime for four hundred years. When the Circle found him out, he started the [[Sundering War]] and tried to tear the shrines down.
- Vale defeated him and died doing it. Wick fled, and his curse froze [[Frostfell]], where his shrine stood.
- Nobody has seen him since. Parents tell children he's still out in the cold, waiting.

## The empty plinth

His statue once stood in the great hall of [[The Emberhall]], a gift of thanks from the city. After the war it was pulled down and his name scratched off the plinth, very carefully, letter by letter. Only the first letter is left under the dust: **W**. [[Brother Oren]] ends his tour there: *this is what betrayal earns.*

In the game today the letter is still an S, from the older story. It becomes a W in Mike's next update.

## The truth

Wick didn't kill the king, and he didn't know who did. For four hundred years he believed, like the rest of the Circle, that the working had simply gone wrong.

**The war:** around year 400 he found out what the [[Radiance Ceremony]] really does, and tried to stop it. Ivy and Reed stood with him. They lost the Sundering War; Ivy and Reed were killed, and Wick was cut off from his shrine.

**Three hundred years in hiding:** Wick is still immortal, but without a shrine he can't siphon, so he's far weaker than [[The Undying]]. He has spent three centuries researching and experimenting, trying to find a way to heal the land and undo the damage. That's why it's taken him so long. Somewhere along the way he began to suspect that Vale killed the king, but he can't prove it.

**The Ashbound:** about twenty years ago he finally made his move. He reached [[Sable]], the Emberguard's brightest champion, before her Ceremony and showed her the truth. She vanished the night before, and together they started [[The Ashbound]]. Sable is its public face. Wick stays hidden.

**In the story:** players meet him at the end of Act 3, after Rhys is drained. In Act 4 he cuts their thread from the Heart Shrine, teaches them to free the great shrines, and gets his strength back when they thaw his own shrine in Frostfell. He fights Vale beside them.

His hope is the [[Ember Seed]]: the thing the Circle's working was meant to make, and in the endgame, the only way to save the Emberborn for good.
