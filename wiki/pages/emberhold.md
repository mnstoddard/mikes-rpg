**Emberhold** is the capital: a walled city on the edge of [[Heartvale]], built around [[The Heart Shrine]] during [[The Rebuilding]]. A great stone wall with towers runs all the way round it. [[The Emberhall]] stands on Ember Hill in the middle, its spire and flame visible from every street.

Since version 0.18 (on the dev test server first) the Prologue happens inside the walls. The four gates stay shut until you've sworn the Oath of the Flame; then they open onto [[Heartvale]], where Act 1 takes place. From outside you see the city's walls, towers and rooftops, with the Emberhall's spire in the middle. Later, homes in the city will be enterable too.

## Districts

- **Ember Hill**: [[The Emberhall]] and its garden, with a plaza below the great steps (the six lanterns of the Prologue's "Light the Way" stand on the steps and round the plaza).
- **The [[Emberhall Training Ground]]**, just east of the Emberhall behind a hedge, where every trainer teaches.
- **Market Square**: market stalls, the warm well (heated from below), the bell tower, [[The General Store]] ([[Shopkeeper Tam]]) and Captain Bryn's bounty board.
- **Forge Row**, to the west: Forester Holt's and Miner Greta's houses, [[Smith Brann's smithy]] and [[Woodworker Rook's workshop]].
- **The Glass River and the docks**, to the east: the river runs through the city under the wall, with wharves, a fish market and trout and salmon to catch. Old Maren's cottage is on the far bank.
- **The Lower Town**, to the south: [[The Hearthstone Inn]], [[The Mages' Guild]], [[Huntmaster Kael's lodge]], [[Herbalist Wren's hut]] and the allotments, where anyone can rent a farm plot from Farmer Odo.

| Gate | Where | Leads to |
|---|---|---|
| North Gate | top of the city | the road north into [[Heartvale]] |
| West Gate | west wall, past Forge Row | the Westroad |
| Saltmere Gate | east wall, over the river | the coast road to [[Saltmere]] |
| River Gate | south wall, by the Lower Town | the river road and the south |

## People

Ordinary townsfolk (Baker Hanna, little Pip, Guard Dunstan and others) walk the streets. Everyone in the game: see [[People in the game]].

People in the game call it Emberhold, and the town history book is "Emberhold: a Town History".
