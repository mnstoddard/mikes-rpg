**The Tangle** is a hot, wet jungle: giant trees, rivers, waterfalls and old [[Aldmera|Aldmeran]] bridges swallowed by roots. Its great shrine stands in a city built up into the canopy. Warden Ivy keeps it ([[Emberwardens]]).

Far from the shrine, the jungle withers by growing too much: vines thicken until they strangle the trees, choke the rivers and pull down whole ruins.

::: secret
Warden Ivy is an impostor. The real Ivy, who raised the shrine, sided with [[Wick]] in the [[Sundering War]] and was killed.
:::
