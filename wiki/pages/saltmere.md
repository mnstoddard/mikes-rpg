**Saltmere Coast** is the harbour town and islands to the east, with the best fishing. Warden Merrow runs its great shrine ([[Emberwardens]]). Ida of the Saltmere was one of [[The Extinguished]].

The Drowned Shrine sank off the coast with its carving: *"The shrines shall close when the war is won."* The glowing fish in its bays are fewer every year.

Not in the game yet; the map shows where it will be.
