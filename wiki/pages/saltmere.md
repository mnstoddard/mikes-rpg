**Saltmere Coast** is the harbour town and islands to the east, with the best fishing. Warden Merrow keeps its great shrine ([[Emberwardens]]). Along the wilder coast, the tides are rising and drowning old towns. Ida of the Saltmere was one of [[The Extinguished]].

The Drowned Shrine sank off the coast with its carving: *"The shrines shall close when the land is healed."* The glowing fish in its bays are fewer every year.

Not in the game yet; the map shows where it will be.
