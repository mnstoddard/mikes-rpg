**Enchanter Vale** is the gentle enchanter in [[The Emberhall]]. People say he was named after the great [[Archmage Vale]]. He never lets anyone touch his staff.

::: secret
He **is** Archmage Vale, over 500 years old, hiding in plain sight. He's also the hooded High Keeper who leads [[The Emberguard]]. Half of the [[Ember Crown]] is set in his staff.
:::
