**Enchanter Corwen** is the gentle enchanter in [[The Emberhall]], east of the Heart Shrine. He was called Enchanter Vale until version 0.22 (Mike's note: Vale's name now belongs to the High Keeper's secret).

::: secret
Like every Keeper, he's in on the Emberguard's secret. He has served [[High Keeper Aldric]] for centuries and knows who Aldric really is: [[Archmage Vale]].
:::
