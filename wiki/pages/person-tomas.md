**Keeper Tomas** keeps the keys of [[The Emberhall]]. He used to sit in the stores by the locked ledger room; since version 0.21 he stands in the great hall, and the stores and ledger room are [[The Restricted Section]], for the Keepers only. Strict and proud of the order. In [[The Hall of Embers]] he teaches new Emberborn about skills and levels, the Emberguard's ranks, and the honour of Radiance: "Build your flame. Never let it dim." He tells Initiates the six [[Emberwardens]] are the highest rank of all. He has noticed the ledger room is opened at night.

::: secret
Like every Keeper, he's in on the secret (version 0.22). He knows the Emberwardens are the old Circle, and what the ledger room holds.
:::
