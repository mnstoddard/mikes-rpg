**Brother Tomas** keeps the keys of [[The Emberhall]]. He used to sit in the stores by the locked ledger room; since version 0.21 he stands in the great hall by the barred door, and the stores and ledger room are [[The Restricted Section]], for the Keepers only. Honest, strict and a true believer. In [[The Hall of Embers]] he teaches new Emberborn about skills and levels, the Emberguard's ranks, and the honour of Radiance: "Build your flame. Never let it dim." He has noticed the ledger room is opened at night.
