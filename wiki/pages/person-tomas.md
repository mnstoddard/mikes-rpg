**Brother Tomas** keeps the keys of [[The Emberhall]]. He used to sit in the stores by the locked ledger room; in the plan he moves out into the great hall, and the stores and ledger room become [[The Restricted Section]], for the Keepers only. Honest, strict and a true believer. In [[The Hall of Embers]] (planned) he teaches new Emberborn about skills and levels, the Emberguard's ranks, and the honour of Radiance: "Build your flame. Never let it dim." He has noticed the ledger room is opened at night.
