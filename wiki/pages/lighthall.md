**The Emberhall** is [[The Emberguard]]'s great white temple in [[Emberhold]], and where every new [[Emberborn]] wakes, on the steps of [[The Heart Shrine]]. The great hall in the middle holds the Heart Shrine's flame, four burning braziers and a long red carpet to the front door. Carved white statues of [[The Ember Circle]] line its walls: [[King Aldemar XVIII]], [[Archmage Vale]], Crowe, Flint, Ivy, Merrow, Dunn and Reed, each on a plinth you can click to read, with [[Wick]]'s empty plinth near the doors. The statues of [[The Extinguished]] stand outside in the Emberhall's garden. Small embers drift up through the hall's air, one for every hero honoured with a [[Radiance Ceremony]], like the embers on the game's title screen. Doors lead off it to the other rooms:

- **The library** (west, north side): shelves of books, [[Keeper Lisette]] and Scholar Quill. [[The Four Books]] sit on its shelves. At the back, an iron grille looks through into the restricted section.
- **The restricted section** (west, south side; since version 0.21): what used to be the stores and the ledger room, now one big locked area for the Keepers only, packed with shelves (version 0.22). The only way in is the grille gate at the back of the library, and Keeper Lisette always has an excuse ready (see [[The Restricted Section]]).
- **The tower stairs** (north-east corner of the great hall; version 0.22): a door up the Emberhall's tall tower to **your room** at the top (see below).
- **The great hall** also gets [[Keeper Tomas]], with his ring of keys, and [[Enchanter Corwen]]'s corner east of the Heart Shrine, which moved out of the old stores (version 0.21).
- **The infirmary** (east): [[Keeper Wynne]] and Healer Rosa, with a corridor and four bedrooms, one for each patient.

Acolytes Bram and Mira sweep the floor and keep the braziers lit, and Pilgrim Ennis comes to pray at the shrine (see [[Townsfolk]]). Kevin MacLeod's "Gregorian Chant" plays softly inside.

A ring-of-fire mark is carved over its front door.

::: secret
It's built right over [[The Ember Circle]]'s old shaft, where the working broke loose. Its sealed stair leads down to the Undying's Sanctum, and further down to [[The Hollow Heart]]. At their Joining, at the end of Act 3, players go down that stair and meet Vale. The drifting embers are scraps of sparks the Ceremonies couldn't fully drain.
:::

## Ember Hill (version 0.22)

The Emberhall stands on **Ember Hill**, a raised stone terrace with battlements round its edge, above the plaza. A wide white stairway climbs from the plaza to the great doors, and the six lanterns of the Prologue's *Light the Way* stand on the stairway's side walls, three each side.

From outside it looks like a castle: round white towers with gold roofs at its four corners, two square gate towers hung with the Emberguard's red and gold banners either side of the entrance, and battlements along the tops of its walls. The front door is a pair of tall arched oak doors with gold bands and studs, in a carved stone arch; they swing open as you walk up. The north-east tower is the tallest, with a lit window at the top: that's your room.

## Your room (version 0.22)

A door in the north-east corner of the great hall climbs the tall tower to a round room at the top, the room every Emberborn is given: a bed, a bedside table, a shelf of books, a fire, a trunk, a round red rug, and an **enchanted chest**. Like every enchanted chest it opens the same storage as all the others, and you can buy more space in it.
