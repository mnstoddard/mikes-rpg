**The Emberhall** is [[The Emberguard]]'s great white temple in [[Emberhold]], and where every new [[Emberborn]] wakes, on the steps of [[The Heart Shrine]]. Inside are the Hall of Champions with the statues of the [[Radiants]], a library ([[Sister Lisette]]), a dormitory ([[Sister Wynne]]), a locked ledger room ([[Brother Tomas]]), and [[Enchanter Vale]]'s corner.

A ring-of-fire mark is carved over its front door.

::: secret
It's built right over [[The Ember Circle]]'s old shaft. Its sealed stair leads down to the Undying's Sanctum. At the Joining, players go down that stair.
:::
