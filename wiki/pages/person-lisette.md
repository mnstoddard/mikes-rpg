**Keeper Lisette** keeps the library in [[The Emberhall]] (see [[Books in the game]]). Honest. In [[The Hall of Embers]] she teaches new Emberborn how quests work, and gives them their first side quest, [[The Four Books]]. She also guards the library's locked restricted section, and always has a reason why nobody can go in today (see [[The Restricted Section]]). A page was cut from the oldest book in her library, and she wants to know who took it.
