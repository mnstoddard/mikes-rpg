**Keeper Lisette** keeps the library in [[The Emberhall]] (see [[Books in the game]]). In [[The Hall of Embers]] she teaches new Emberborn how quests work, and gives them their first side quest, [[The Four Books]]. She also guards the library's locked restricted section, and always has a reason why nobody can go in today (see [[The Restricted Section]]). A page was cut from the oldest book in her library, and she wants to know who took it.

::: secret
Like every Keeper, she's in on the Emberguard's secret (version 0.22). She has read everything in the restricted section, and every excuse she gives for keeping you out of it is a lie she knows is a lie (see [[The Restricted Section]]).
:::
