**Sister Lisette** keeps the library in [[The Emberhall]] (see [[Books in the game]]). Honest. In [[The Hall of Embers]] (planned) she teaches new Emberborn how quests work, and gives them their first side quest, [[The Four Books]]. A page was cut from the oldest book in her library, and she wants to know who took it.
