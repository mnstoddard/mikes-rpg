**The Mages' Guild** are scholars of magic and soul stone. Their hall in [[Emberhold]] has the enchanting altar, the alchemy table, enchanted chests you can rent, and a board of magic jobs (see [[Bounties]]). [[Archmage Rowan]] runs it and teaches [[Enchanting]]; [[Alchemist Moss]] teaches [[Alchemy]] there.

Its records about [[The Heartfire]] stop around year 200. Scholars find that odd.

::: secret
The Guild is honest, but the soul stone it sells ends up feeding [[The Undying]]. Its missing records were taken by the Emberguard.
:::
