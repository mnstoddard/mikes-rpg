**Heartvale** is a round valley of open grassland, farms and rivers, and the first region players see. Its great shrine is [[The Heart Shrine]], in the Emberhall. [[Emberhold]] sits on its edge; [[The Thornwood]] lies to the south behind a fenced gate, [[Greyspine Peaks]] to the north.

It's round because the ground fell in at [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]]. [[The Emberheart]] burns far below it, in [[The Hollow Heart]], the main story's final area. Out at the valley's edges, far from the Heart Shrine, the grass is turning to grey dust.

Heartvale is the big outdoor map: the old village, the lake, the pasture, the farm, the quarry and the edge of the Thornwood. Since version 0.17 players start inside the walls of [[Emberhold]], and Heartvale lies past its gates, which are shut for now.

Out here (and in the houses, shops and caves) the music is "Realm of Exploration", the world song Mike chose. It repeats without a break. Only the [[Emberhall]] has music of its own.
