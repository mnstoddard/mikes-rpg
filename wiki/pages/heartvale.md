**Heartvale** is a green valley of farms and rivers, and the first region players see. [[Emberhold]] sits on its edge; [[The Thornwood]] lies to the south behind a fenced gate, [[Greyspine Peaks]] to the north.

It's round because the ground fell in at [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]]. [[The Heartfire]] burns far below it.

In the game today, Heartvale is the whole outdoor map: the town, the lake, Trainer Row, the pasture, the farm, the quarry and the edge of the Thornwood.
