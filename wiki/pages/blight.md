**The Blight** is the name for sick, angry animals: grey, glowing and mean. The [[Hollow wolf|Hollow wolves]] of Wolf Hollow are Blighted. Everyone believes [[The shrines]] keep the Blight back.

::: secret
Blighted creatures are living things the ember has drained out of. They're starving for ember, and that's what makes them angry. The Blight starts at the small shrines: a Blighted fox's den is right under one.
:::
