**The Blight** is the name for sick, angry animals: grey, glowing and mean. The [[Hollow wolf|Hollow wolves]] of Wolf Hollow are Blighted. Everyone believes [[The shrines]] keep the Blight back.

::: secret
Blighted creatures are living things the ember has drained out of. They're starving for ember, and that's what makes them angry. The Blight starts at the cold edges, furthest from any shrine, and creeps inward with the Withering. Blighted animals often come toward the shrines, drawn to the warmth they can't get enough of.
:::
