**Embervale** is the land the game is set in. The game itself is called **Emberfall**, after [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]], the disaster 800 years ago that rained fire across the land and destroyed the kingdom of [[Aldmera]].

Deep under the land lies the source of its warmth, which people call [[The Emberheart]]. That warmth, [[The ember|the ember]], is in everything that grows.

## The story in one paragraph

Eight hundred years ago, King Aldemar XVIII and his [[The Ember Circle|Ember Circle]] tried to harness the Emberheart for the whole kingdom. As they finished, [[Archmage Vale]] murdered the king and twisted the working to fill himself with power. It broke loose, and Aldmera fell. The Circle, believing it had simply gone wrong, built [[The shrines]] to make the [[Emberborn]], protectors to save the land. But the power corrupted them. Vale created a Ceremony that drains the oldest heroes into himself, destroyed the members who found out, and rewrote history so that [[Wick]], his old friend, is blamed for everything. Today the Circle rules in secret as [[The Undying]], behind [[The Emberguard]].

Players are Emberborn. They start as [[Hopefuls]], trying to prove themselves worthy of joining the Emberguard. On the day they're invited in, they learn the truth and choose: join the Emberguard and live forever on stolen fire, or join [[The Ashbound]], Wick's hidden resistance, who are trying to free the land even if it costs every Emberborn their life.

> Would you live forever on something stolen, or give up your life so the land can live?
