**Embervale** is the land the game is set in. The game itself is called **Emberfall**, after [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]], the disaster 500 years ago that rained fire across Embervale and broke the [[Old Kingdom]].

Deep under the land burns its own heart, a living fire called [[The Emberheart]]. Its warmth, [[The ember|the ember]], is in everything that grows.

## The story in one paragraph

Long ago a circle of dark mages broke the earth open to drink the Emberheart and live forever. They caused the Emberfall, then slowly rewrote history until everyone believed they were the land's protectors: [[The Emberguard]].

Players are [[Emberborn]], heroes made from that stolen ember. They start as [[Hopefuls]], trying to prove themselves worthy of joining the Emberguard. On the day they're invited in, they learn the truth and choose: join the Emberguard and live forever on stolen fire, or join [[The Ashbound]], who are shutting the siphon down even though every Emberborn, themselves included, will turn to ash when it stops.

> Would you live forever on something stolen, or give up your life so the land can live?
