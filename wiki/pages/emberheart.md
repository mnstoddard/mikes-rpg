**The Emberheart** is what people call the source of the land's warmth: a deep fire far under [[Heartvale]] that sends [[The ember|ember]] up through the rock and soil into everything that grows. It isn't a heart in the literal sense. The name is old, from the [[Aldmere]], who thought of it as the heart of the land.

[[The Ember Circle]] found it and tried to harness it for all [[Aldmera]], and that working broke loose as [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]]. It burns in [[The Hollow Heart]], a lake of living fire with thousands of thin threads rising from it toward [[The shrines]]. The closest you can get to it on foot without going that deep is the [[The crystal cave|Crystal Cave]], where the rock is warm to the touch.

::: secret
Eight hundred years of shrines pulling ember toward the cities, and toward [[The Undying]], have left it weaker. It isn't going out, but the land far from the shrines is going cold ([[The Withering]]), and [[Miner Greta]] says the Crystal Cave's rock isn't as warm as when she was a girl. [[Wick]]'s research is about giving back what was taken.
:::
