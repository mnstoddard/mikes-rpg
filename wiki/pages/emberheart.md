**The Emberheart** is the land's heart: a fire that has always burned deep under [[Heartvale]]. It beats slowly, about once a minute, and every beat sends warmth up through the rock and soil. That warmth is [[The ember|the ember]]. It's why seeds sprout, springs run warm, and animals have the strength to live.

The closest you can get to it on foot is the [[The crystal cave|Crystal Cave]], where you can hear it beat in the rock. It burns in [[The Hollow Heart]], a lake of living fire with thousands of thin threads rising from it, one for every [[Emberborn]].

## Is it alive?

Nobody knows for sure.

::: secret
It's alive in the way a tree is alive. It can't think or talk, but it can be hurt, and it is weakening, because [[The shrines]] have been draining it for 460 years. If the Emberheart goes out, the whole land goes cold and dies. [[Frostfell]]'s ice is already creeping south, and the heartbeat in the Crystal Cave is slower than it was when [[Miner Greta]] was a girl.
:::
