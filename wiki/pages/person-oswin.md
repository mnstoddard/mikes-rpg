**Repairer Oswin** mends worn tools, weapons and armor for a few coins. *"Mending always costs something."* He's [[The Rooted|Rooted]].

::: secret
He has loved [[Old Maren]] for 50 years. An old lantern he asks you to fix holds a note in Maren's writing (the planned quest *Mending Costs*). What it says is waiting on Maren's new story.
:::
