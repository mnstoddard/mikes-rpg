**Repairer Oswin** mends worn tools, weapons and armor for a few coins. *"Mending always costs something."* He's [[The Rooted|Rooted]].

::: secret
He has loved [[Old Maren]] for 50 years. An old lantern he asks you to fix holds a note in Maren's writing (the planned quest *Mending Costs*). What it says is waiting on Maren's new story.
:::

## Repairing (version 0.22, Mike's note)

Repairing is magic, not smithing. Even things that seem lifeless came from the land, and hold a little ember. Oswin coaxes the ember out of a **spare of the same kind** and into your worn one: any axe can mend an axe, any pickaxe a pickaxe, any sword a sword. A spare as good as yours or better mends it fully; a plainer one only mends a little (a bronze axe barely helps a mithril one). He picks the plainest spare in your bag that does the job, and charges a few coins.

> Worn out? Broken? Everything comes from the land, you know, and holds a little ember. Bring me a spare of the same kind, any axe for an axe, and I'll coax its ember into your worn one for a few coins. A plain one only gives a little to a fine one, mind.

The **Mend** spell does the same thing yourself, with no coins. It's why a player might make a pile of plain bronze axes: to keep a rare one going instead of replacing it.
