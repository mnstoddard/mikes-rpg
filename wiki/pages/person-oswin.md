**Repairer Oswin** mends worn tools, weapons and armor for a few coins. *"Mending always costs something."* He's [[The Rooted|Rooted]].

::: secret
He has loved [[Old Maren]] for 50 years, and helps her. An old lantern he asks you to fix holds a 70-year-old note in Maren's writing: *"I've done it. I'm free. It's started."*
:::
