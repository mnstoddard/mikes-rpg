**The Graveyard** lies in [[The Thornwood]]. It's a place for night herbs, and ghosts.

::: secret
The ghosts are drained [[Emberborn]], still tied to the old Thornwood shrine. One counts to eleven over and over: she was Halric the Dawnbringer's friend (see [[Radiants]]).
:::
