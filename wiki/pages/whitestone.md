**Whitestone** was the capital of [[Aldmera]], a city of white stone in the west, seat of the Aldemar kings. The torrent of [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]] tore across the land and broke it, and the farmland around it burned into [[The Ashen Barrens]]. Its ruins are called [[The Sundered Crown]]. A broken milestone on the King's Road still reads *"Whitestone, 40 miles."*

It used to be called Brightcrown in the wiki.
