**The Sundering War** was the hundred-year war that split [[The Ember Circle]], from about year 400 to 500. Circle members fought each other with all the power of their shrines, and the land suffered for it.

## What everyone is taught

[[Wick]] turned Ivy and Reed against the rest of the Circle and tried to tear the shrines down, which would have let the Emberfall loose again and made the king's sacrifice for nothing. [[Archmage Vale]] led the loyal Circle against him and won, and died doing it. Ivy and Reed were defeated. Wick fled, and his curse froze [[Frostfell]].

[[Keeper Oren]] tells this version on his tour of [[The Extinguished]], ending at Wick's empty plinth.

::: secret
## What really happened

Around year 400, Wick grew suspicious of the [[Radiance Ceremony]] and found out what it does: it doesn't give a champion's ember to new heroes, it pours it into Vale. He tried to stop the Ceremonies, and the Circle split.

- **With Vale:** Crowe, Flint, Merrow and Dunn. They had grown used to the power their shrines brought them, and chose to keep it.
- **With Wick:** Ivy and Reed.

Vale's side won. Ivy and Reed were killed. Wick survived, but the victors took his shrine and cut him off from it, and Frostfell froze with it.

**The impostors:** nobody outside the Circle ever learned that Ivy and Reed had died. Vale chose two of the most corrupt Emberguard and Emberborn to take their names and their shrines. Today's Warden Ivy and Warden Reed are those impostors, living on the siphon like the rest of [[The Undying]].

**The rewrite:** at the end of the war Vale faked his death and began rewriting history, so that Wick became the one who caused the Emberfall too. The Emberguard took [[The Mages' Guild]]'s records about the Emberheart around this time.
