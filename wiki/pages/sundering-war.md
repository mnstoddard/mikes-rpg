**The Sundering War** followed [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]]. The kingdom's lords went to war over the glowing shards the Emberfall left behind. [[King Aldemar]] forged the [[Ember Crown]] from the biggest shard and used it as a weapon, and the blast tore the capital, [[Brightcrown]], apart. That blast made the great western crater, [[The Sundered Crown]]. The Old Kingdom fell, and the west burned into [[The Ashen Barrens]].

In year 40 the first [[Emberborn]] woke at [[The shrines|the new shrines]] and won the war.

People today say *"nobody remembers how the war began."* [[Brother Oren]] tells the Emberguard's version on his tour of the statues of [[The Extinguished]].
