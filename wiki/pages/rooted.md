**The Rooted** are the ordinary folk of the land: people born the usual way, who can't come back from death. Most of Emberhold's townsfolk are Rooted, and they trust [[The Emberguard]] completely. [[Sister Celene]], [[Repairer Oswin]] and [[Tamsin]] are Rooted.
