A **Radiance Ceremony** is the Emberguard's highest honour. Every few decades their greatest champion is chosen, and at the Ceremony they "go into the light". Their statue stands in the Hall of Champions in [[The Lighthall]] (see [[Radiants]]).

::: secret
The champion is drained. Their whole spark is poured into [[The Undying]], which feeds them for years. A few sparks are still trapped in old soul gems. Only [[Sable]] ever survived it.
:::
