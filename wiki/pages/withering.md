**The Withering** is what happens to land that has lost its [[The ember|ember]]. Grass goes brown, trees turn black, and animals become [[The Blight|Blighted]]. In [[The Thornwood]] it shows as the Dark Forest, which is spreading toward [[Emberhold]] a few steps a day.

It started in [[The Ashen Barrens]], burned in the [[Sundering War]].

People say the Blight is just bad luck, and that [[The Ashbound]] make it worse by smashing shrines.

::: secret
The Withering spreads out from [[The shrines]]. The shrines pull ember out of the land around them, and the land goes cold. Trees die nearest the shrines first, [[Herbalist Wren]]'s maps show the Withering spreading from them, and [[Huntmaster Kael]] says the Blight is always worst downhill of a shrine.
:::
