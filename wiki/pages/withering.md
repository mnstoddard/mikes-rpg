**The Withering** is what happens to land that has lost its [[The ember|ember]]. The cities around the great [[The shrines|shrines]] are warm and thriving, but out in the wild, far from them, the land is going cold, and animals become [[The Blight|Blighted]]. Each region withers in its own way:

| Region | How it withers |
|---|---|
| [[Heartvale]] | Grass turns to grey dust |
| [[The Thornwood]] | The forest rots and goes dark: the Dark Forest, spreading toward [[Emberhold]] a few steps a day |
| [[Greyspine Peaks]] | Eruptions, and the grey sickness |
| [[The Tangle]] | Vines that strangle everything |
| [[Saltmere Coast]] | Drowning tides |
| [[The Ashen Barrens]] | Glass and sand |
| [[Blackfen]] | Black water and fog |
| [[Frostfell]] | Ice, creeping south |

People say the Withering is just bad luck, and lately the Emberguard say [[The Ashbound]] are spreading it.

::: secret
[[The shrines]] aren't the cause. They still warm the land around them, but [[Archmage Vale]] pulls far too much ember down their threads, so each shrine has less to give and its warmth reaches less far every year. The Withering starts at the far edges, furthest from any shrine, and creeps inward toward them. The cities, right beside the great shrines, feel nothing.

The clues all point the same way: something is taking the warmth before it can reach the edges.

- [[Forester Holt]]'s count: the trees near the shrines are fine, almost every tree out at the valley's edge is dead, and his father's count forty years ago found the edge still green.
- [[Herbalist Wren]]'s maps, drawn every ten years, show the grey ring tightening around the shrines.
- [[Huntmaster Kael]]: the Blight is always worst furthest from a shrine, and the line moves closer every year.
- [[Farmer Odo]]: a blessing lasted a whole season in his gran's day, and barely a week now.
- A relit shrine's warmth is pulled back down its thread toward [[Heartvale]] (see [[Main story]]).

It began centuries before the Ashbound existed.
:::
