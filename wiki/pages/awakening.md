## Planned version

The first quest teaches one thing at a time. While you get dressed, **nothing on the screen works except the 🎒 bag button**, and it glows. You can't walk, turn the camera, talk to anyone or open any other panel until both robe pieces are on.

### Step 1: Talk to Sister Celene, beside the Heart Shrine

You wake lying on the shrine steps. Celene comes over and helps you up.

**Sister Celene:**

> Easy, easy. Take my hand. Up you come. There you are.
>
> You've just woken on the steps of the Heart Shrine. Its flame flared up, the embers gathered, and there you were. That's how all of you come to us: made by the Emberheart itself.
>
> Do you remember your name? Anything at all? No? Don't be frightened. None of you ever do.
>
> Now, you can't go about in nothing but your underthings. I've put a robe top and a robe skirt in your bag. See it glowing, down in the corner? Open it.

**You get:** a robe top and a robe skirt, straight into your bag.

### Step 2: Put on your robe, one piece at a time

- **The screen is locked.** Only the glowing 🎒 bag button can be pressed. Clicking anywhere else does nothing (a small wobble on the bag button shows where to go).
- **Open the bag.** The bag opens, and the robe top glows.
- **Click the robe top.** The **Equip** button glows. Press it, and the robe top appears on your character.
- **Then the robe skirt glows.** Click it and press **Equip**.

The quest log shows a checklist, ticked as you go (the order doesn't matter, so either piece can go on first):

- ☐ Robe top
- ☐ Robe skirt

**Sister Celene, when both are on:**

> There! It suits you. Now you look like one of the Emberborn.
>
> Everything you carry lives in that bag, and everything you wear shows on you. You'll be in and out of it all day.
>
> Let's see you walk; your legs will feel new for a while. See the two glowing marks on the floor, down the hall? Walk to each of them.

Now the screen unlocks.

### Step 3: Walk to the two glowing marks in the hall

As in the game today: click or tap the floor, use W A S D, or the round pad on a touch screen. Checklist, any order:

- ☐ Left mark
- ☐ Right mark

**Sister Celene:**

> Look at you, walking like you've done it all your life! Come and meet Keeper Aldric, beside the Heart Shrine. He'll want to welcome you himself.

### Step 4: Talk to Keeper Aldric

**Keeper Aldric:**

> Welcome, friend! Welcome to the Emberhall, and to Emberhold.
>
> The Emberheart made you for a reason. You won't remember anything; none of you do. We call you the Emberborn: heroes the shrines make from the land's own ember, when the land needs them. Until you've proven yourself, you're an Initiate.
>
> And the land does need you. The Withering creeps in from the edges of the land, the Ashbound smash our shrines, and the Thornwood grows darker every year.
>
> But not today. Today you're safe, and everything here is yours to learn.

### What changes from the game today

- Step 2 locks everything except the bag, and makes the bag, then each robe piece, then the Equip button glow in turn.
- Celene puts the robe in your bag and points at it, instead of telling you where the bag button is.
- The robe pieces show as a checklist in the quest log.
- Aldric says Initiate, not Hopeful, and the Withering "creeps in from the edges of the land" (see [[The Withering]]).
