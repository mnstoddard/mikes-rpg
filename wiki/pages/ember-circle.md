**The Ember Circle** was a small group of [[Aldmera]]'s greatest mages, led by [[King Aldemar XVIII]] himself. They studied [[The ember]], found its source in [[The Emberheart]], and set out to harness it for the whole kingdom. That working broke loose in year 0: [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]]. 🔒 (Nobody today is taught about the working. The official story is that the land's fire simply woke.)

The surviving members came out of the Fall filled with power and no longer ageing. Each raised a great shrine in their own region (see [[The shrines]]), and together they led [[The Rebuilding]] and founded [[The Emberguard]].

Their mark is a ring of fire, the ring they stood in around the pit. It's on a ruined mage tower in the Barrens, and carved over [[The Emberhall]]'s front door.

Their statues line the great hall of [[The Emberhall]]: the king, Vale, Crowe, Flint, Ivy, Merrow, Dunn and Reed, carved in white stone, and Wick's empty plinth near the doors. [[Brother Oren]] gives the tour.

## What really happened to them

The Circle meant well. Only Vale knew that he'd twisted the working; the others believed it had simply gone terribly wrong, and built the shrines to make the Emberborn and put the land right.

But they lived on the power their shrines pulled in, and over two centuries it corrupted them. When Vale created the [[Radiance Ceremony]] around year 200, the others looked away. Around year 400 Wick found out what the Ceremony really does, and the Circle split: the [[Sundering War]]. Vale's side won. Ivy and Reed were killed, and Wick was cut off from his shrine and driven into hiding.

After the war the survivors became [[The Undying]]. They let the world believe the Circle had passed into legend, and took up the Emberwardens' posts as if they were heirs carrying old titles. The impostors in Ivy's and Reed's places did the same, and nobody has ever known the difference.

All nine names are still carved on the frozen shrine in [[Frostfell]]. Wick's shrine was the one place the victors never scratched them off.
