**The Sundered Crown** is what's left of [[Whitestone]], the capital of [[Aldmera]]: a broken city of white stone, half buried in glass and ash, past the far western edge of [[The Ashen Barrens]]. It's the biggest ruin in the land, the most dangerous place in it, and the game's endgame, for after the main story is done. People call it the Sundered Crown because the kingdom's crown city was split apart in the Emberfall.

::: secret
The royal Crown Vault under its ruins holds the [[Ember Seed]] and the other half of the [[Ember Crown]]. In the endgame, players search the ruins for the Seed, while the impostor wardens Ivy and Reed hunt the Crown half to bring Vale back (see [[Main story]]).
:::
