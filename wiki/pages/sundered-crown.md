**The Sundered Crown** is the endgame zone, a crater of ruins in the far west. It's what's left of [[Brightcrown]], where the [[Ember Crown]] went off in the [[Sundering War]].

::: secret
Half of the Ember Crown and the [[Ember Seed]] are hidden in its Crown Vault. Both sides search the ruins: the Emberguard for the Crown, the Ashbound for the Seed.
:::
