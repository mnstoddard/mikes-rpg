**The Sundered Crown** is what's left of [[Whitestone]], the capital of [[Aldmera]]: a broken city of white stone, half buried in glass and sand, deep in [[The Ashen Barrens]]. It's the biggest ruin in the land, and one of the most dangerous places in it. People call it the Sundered Crown because the kingdom's crown city was split apart in the Emberfall.

::: secret
The [[Ember Seed]] is hidden in the royal Crown Vault under its ruins. In the endgame the Ashbound search here for it, while the Emberguard hunt for the other half of the [[Ember Crown]] in [[The Hollow Heart]].
:::
