**The Hearthwise** were the old folk of the [[Old Kingdom]] who lived by the one rule of [[The ember]]: *"Take only what the land gives back."* Their saying is still painted over a few old doorways.

Their ways live on in odd places. The goblins of [[Greyspine Peaks]] sing a Hearthwise song to keep the grey sickness away, and [[The Ashbound]] build hearths that burn without a shrine, the Hearthwise way.
