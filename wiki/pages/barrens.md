**The Ashen Barrens** are dead badlands in the west: ruins of the [[Old Kingdom]], the King's Road, and [[The Ashbound]]'s camp. Warden Dunn runs its great shrine ([[Emberwardens]]).

It burned in the [[Sundering War]], and [[The Withering]] started here. [[Tamsin]]'s village withered away here.

Not in the game yet; the road west is closed.
