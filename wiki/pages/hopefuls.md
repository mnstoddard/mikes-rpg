Every player starts as a **Hopeful**: a new [[Emberborn]] who isn't a member of [[The Emberguard]] yet. The Emberguard welcome Hopefuls kindly, but each is one of hundreds. To be allowed to try for the Emberguard, a Hopeful swears the Oath of the Flame, then earns trial marks across the land: Ember, Flame, Blaze and Radiant (see [[Trial marks]]).

When a Hopeful has proven themselves, the Emberguard invite them to join. That's the day they learn the truth (see [[Main story]]).
