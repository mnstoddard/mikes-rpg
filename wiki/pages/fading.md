**The Fading** is what happens to [[Emberborn]] who live long enough. They don't age, but they wear thin. Every fall, every rebirth and every [[The Tithe|Tithe]] takes a little. After a few hundred years an Emberborn grows tired, colours look grey, and old memories slip. Everyone in Embervale has watched an old champion fade, and nobody wants to end that way.

It first showed in the earliest Emberborn around year 200, and it's why [[Archmage Vale]] created the [[Radiance Ceremony]]: a way to lay the flame down with honour before the Fading takes you.

The Fading is real. That's what makes the Ceremony easy to believe in.

::: secret
The Emberguard speed it up for the champions they want. Blessings, Tithes and praise are tuned to wear the brightest down faster, so a champion full of ember "feels the Fading" a century early. The dim and ordinary are never invited to a Ceremony; their ember isn't worth the harvest.
:::
