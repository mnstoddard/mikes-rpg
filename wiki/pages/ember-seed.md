**The Ember Seed** is what [[The Ember Circle]]'s great working was meant to make: a seed of pure ember that, planted in the land, would warm it gently forever, giving without taking. It was [[King Aldemar XVIII]]'s dream for [[Aldmera]], and the sign of his house (a seed under a crown) is on every Aldmeran coin.

The working broke before the Seed was planted. The half-made Seed was kept in the royal vault at [[Whitestone]], and it's still there, in the Crown Vault under [[The Sundered Crown]].

[[Wick]] has spent three hundred years working out how to finish it. Planted at [[The Emberheart]], it would heal the land, and let [[Emberborn]] live on their own fire without the shrines. After Vale falls in Act 4, it's the only way to save the Emberborn for good.

## In the endgame

1. **Find it:** players fight their way into the Crown Vault, the first big endgame dungeon.
2. **Finish it:** Wick's research says it needs a piece of each region's healed land, such as Thornwood heartwood, Greyspine fire-glass and Saltmere pearl, each from hard endgame content in that region.
3. **Plant it** at the Emberheart. This can be saved for a later update. Wick may have to give up what's left of his power to make it take root.

See [[Main story]].
