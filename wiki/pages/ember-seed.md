**The Ember Seed** is a pure ember from before [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]], hidden in the Crown Vault under [[The Sundered Crown]]. Planted in [[The Emberheart]], it might let [[Emberborn]] live on their own fire, without the siphon. It's [[The Ashbound]]'s one hope, and the Emberguard want it so nothing ever replaces the siphon.

Hints: [[Farmer Odo]]'s jar of seed from before the Emberfall, and Old Kingdom coins showing a seed under a crown.
:::
