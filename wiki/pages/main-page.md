Welcome to the **Emberfall Wiki**. It's the place to look things up and keep the game consistent: who someone is, what an item does, where a monster lives, what really happened 500 years ago.

Pages come in two kinds, and each page says which it is at the bottom:

- **Written pages** hold the story: the lore, places, factions and people. They follow the lore bible.
- **Pages made from the game's code** hold the facts: every monster, item, skill, spell, quest, shop and book, with the real numbers. They're rebuilt from the game every time, so they always match what's live.

Red links are pages that don't exist yet. Secrets (the twists players shouldn't learn early) are in red boxes; turn off 🔒 *Show secrets* at the top to read the wiki the way a player would.
