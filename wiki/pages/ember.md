**The ember** is the land's own warmth. It comes from [[The Heartfire]] and is in everything that grows.

## Where it shows

Where the ember runs close to the surface, you can see it.

- Ore glows. Soul stone is ember that cooled into crystal.
- The old trees of [[The Thornwood]] glowed softly at night.
- A few people are born with more of it than others, and can shape it with their minds: they become mages. Mana is just ember you can use.
- Embers that landed in rock during [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]] cooled into glowing shards worth more than gold.

## The one rule

The land gives a little ember to everything, and takes it back when things die and rot. It's a circle. Take more than the land gives back, and that spot goes cold. Where it goes cold, things turn grey, sick and dark. That is [[The Withering]], [[The Blight]], and the spreading Dark Forest.

The old folk who lived by the rule were called [[The Hearthwise]]. Their saying is still painted over a few old doorways: *"Take only what the land gives back."*
