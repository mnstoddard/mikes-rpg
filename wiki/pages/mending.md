**The Mending** is the work of healing the land, and it never really ends. Every region has repeatable Mending quests at every level, open to everyone, wherever they are in the [[Main story]].

## What players do

- Clear Blighted beasts from the withering lands.
- Plant [[The Tenders|Tender]] gardens and tend them.
- Break [[The Withering]]'s hold on a ruin, a grove or a stretch of shore.
- Each region has its own kind: cutting back the vines in [[The Tangle]], clearing the fog in [[Blackfen]], shoring up villages against the tides on [[Saltmere Coast]], and so on.

## The land comes back

Each player has a Mending score in each region. As it grows, that region visibly heals for them: grass returns, trees glow again, the fog lifts, the ice pulls back.

Before Act 4, the Emberguard hand out the Mending as "fighting the Blight". After Act 4, when the great shrines have been freed, the land heals faster, and Mending quests also gather what the [[Ember Seed]] needs.
