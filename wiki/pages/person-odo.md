**Farmer Odo** teaches [[Farming]] and sells seeds and plots at the farm east of town. He won't take blessings: in his gran's day one lasted a season, and now it barely lasts a week. "The shrines have less to give every year. I'd rather not lean on 'em." ([[Odo's Stubborn Field]]) He keeps a jar of seed from before the Emberfall.
