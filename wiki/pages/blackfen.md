**Blackfen** is a swamp of reeds, slow rivers and drowned forest, with a town on stilts around its great shrine. Warden Reed keeps it ([[Emberwardens]]).

Far from the shrine, the water turns black and still, and a cold fog rises that never lifts. People who go into it lose their way.

::: secret
Warden Reed is an impostor. The real Reed, who raised the shrine, sided with [[Wick]] in the [[Sundering War]] and was killed.
:::
