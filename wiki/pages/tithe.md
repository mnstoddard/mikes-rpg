**The Tithe** (also called Ember Debt) is what an [[Emberborn]] pays each time they fall and come back. The Emberguard teach that it's a small, fair price for being made again, and [[Alchemist Moss]] brews "Tithe tonics" for [[The Emberhall]] to clear it.

::: secret
The Tithe is the shrine skimming a little ember off you, sending it on to [[The Undying]]. What makes it possible is the [[Oath of the Flame]]: the Oath is really a binding spell. Before you swear it, a fall costs you nothing. After it, every fall pays the Tithe, and a share of everything you learn flows down your thread to the Circle. Players stop paying in Act 4, when Wick breaks their Oath.
:::
