**The Ashbound** are known in [[Emberhold]] as mad bandits who smash shrines and attack heroes. [[Captain Bryn]] puts bounties on them because the Emberguard ask. In the game today you meet Ashbound scouts on the Westroad and Ashbound raiders deep in [[The Thornwood]].

They pass messages hidden inside the bases of [[Woodworker Rook]]'s shrine totems.

## What the Emberguard say

For most of their twenty-odd years, the Ashbound were just "bandits in the woods". A few years ago that changed. The Emberguard now teach that the Ashbound are led by [[Sable]], the champion whose flawed spark the Heart Shrine refused, and that they're the ones killing the land: poisoning the ground round the shrines, spreading the Withering out of the west and the grey sickness through the trees. Every dead field gets blamed on them.

::: secret
## How they were founded

Around year 470, [[Sable]], brought back as a new Emberborn after her Ceremony, finally remembered enough to understand what she'd felt for three hundred years: the shrines pulling ember out of [[The Emberheart]], and the Ceremonies feeding [[The Undying]]. She gathered the few who'd believe her and founded the Ashbound. The Emberguard didn't learn who their leader was until about year 495.

The Withering began centuries before the Ashbound existed. Blaming them is the Emberguard's cover-up for what the shrines are doing.
:::

## Why they're called the Ashbound

[[Emberborn]] only exist because of the siphon. If every shrine goes dark, every Emberborn crumbles to ash, the Ashbound's own fighters included. The Ashbound know this, and they smash shrines anyway, so the land can live. They are bound for ash, and they chose it. Their saying is *"Better ash than a thief."*

::: secret
They're the good guys: Emberborn and [[The Rooted]] who know the truth about [[The shrines]], shutting the siphon down. Their hope is the [[Ember Seed]]. They live the [[The Hearthwise|Hearthwise]] way, with hearths that burn without a shrine.
:::
