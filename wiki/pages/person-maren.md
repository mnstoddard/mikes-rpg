**Old Maren** lives in [[Old Maren's cottage|her cottage]] by the Glass River in [[Emberhold]], and teaches by the pond in the [[Emberhall Training Ground]]. She's a kindly old woman, [[The Rooted|Rooted]], who looks out for the new [[Emberborn]]: a warm meal, a bit of advice, a place to sit when the Emberhall feels too big. Everyone in town has a Maren story.

She is not an Emberborn. Her own story is still being decided.

In the game today she's the fishing and cooking teacher. That role came from the older story, and may change in Mike's next update.

## Her lessons (version 0.22)

*Living off the land*, step by step:

1. Catch a minnow by hand at the pond, and bring it to her.
2. She gives you her old pine rod, nearly worn out. Equip it and fish until it breaks.
3. **Breaking:** she explains that every tool wears down each time you use it (weapons when you hit, armor when you're hit), and works just as well right up until it breaks. A broken tool only takes up room, so she shows you how to throw it away: click it in your bag and press 🗑️ **Trash**.
4. She lends you her good **oak rod**: *"It's only on loan, mind! Catch me 10 more fish with it and it's yours to keep."* Ten fish later, it's yours.
5. Your Minnow Mastery went up: she shows you how to spend a Mastery point in the 📊 panel's 🌳 skill trees.
6. Cooking: your first stew always burns. Then she gives you **one** bronze pot, at full durability (no spare), and the second stew cooks.
7. Eat the stew. If your stamina is already full, the game tells you to catch a few fish first to use some up.
8. Reward: 2 stews and 50 coins, and she sends you to [[Smith Brann]] first.
