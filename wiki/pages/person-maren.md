**Old Maren** lives in [[Old Maren's cottage|her cottage]] on the lake shore. She teaches every new hero the basics of living off the land (fishing, cooking, gathering), and everyone in town says she's been here forever. Her grey hair drifts like ash.

::: secret
Maren is the very first [[Emberborn]], woken in year 40. Around year 430 she learned the truth about [[The shrines]] and cut her own thread. Since then she's been slowly ageing and turning to ash: her grey hair is ash, and it drifts off her fingertips. She quietly warns the heroes who are ready ([[Maren's Question]]). [[Repairer Oswin]] has loved her for 50 years and helps her. Her old cookbook ends: *"Some fires should go out. Remember that."*
:::
