Every main-story quest in order, from waking up to planting the Seed. Built quests are in the game today and link to their walkthroughs. Planned ones are the story's plan (see [[Main story]]). Side quests are on [[Quests]] and [[Planned quests]]. Rows marked 🔒 are secrets, hidden when Show secrets is off.

On claude.ai you can filter this list, and move quests up and down to try a new order. Then press **Save order as a note** to send the new order to Claude.
