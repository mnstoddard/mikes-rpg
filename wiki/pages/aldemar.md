**King Aldemar** was the last king of the [[Old Kingdom]]. In the [[Sundering War]] he forged the [[Ember Crown]] from the biggest Emberfall shard and used it as a weapon. Its blast destroyed his own capital, [[Brightcrown]].

His statue lies buried in [[The Ashen Barrens]]. In it he holds a crown with a glowing stone in it (a planned side quest, *Aldemar's Statue*).
