**High Keeper Aldric** is warm and cheerful. He welcomes every new [[Emberborn]] at [[The Emberhall]], gives the Prologue and Act 1 quests, and decides who is ready for [[The Emberguard]]'s trials. He wrote *On the Shrines* (see [[Books in the game]]).

::: secret
He **is** [[Archmage Vale]], over 800 years old, hiding in plain sight as the cheerful old man who greets every new Emberborn. The man whose twist broke the working and cost [[King Aldemar XVIII]] his life, who faked his death after the [[Sundering War]], and who leads [[The Undying]]. Half of the [[Ember Crown]] is set in his staff. He has been welcoming Initiates, and judging which of them will burn brightest, for as long as anyone remembers. Every Keeper knows exactly what the Radiance Ceremonies do.
:::
