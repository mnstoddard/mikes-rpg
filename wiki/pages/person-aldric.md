**Keeper Aldric** is warm and cheerful. He welcomes every new [[Emberborn]] at [[The Emberhall]], gives the Prologue and Act 1 quests, and decides who is ready for [[The Emberguard]]'s trials. He wrote *On the Shrines* (see [[Books in the game]]).

::: secret
He's one of [[The Undying]]: not a member of the old Circle, but one of the Emberguard Vale rewarded after the [[Sundering War]] with a share of the siphon. He has been welcoming Initiates, and judging which of them will burn brightest, for three hundred years.
:::
