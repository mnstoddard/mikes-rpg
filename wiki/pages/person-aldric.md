**Keeper Aldric** is warm and cheerful. He welcomes every new [[Emberborn]] at [[The Lighthall]], gives the Prologue and Act 1 quests, and decides who is ready for [[The Emberguard]]'s trials. He wrote *On the Shrines* (see [[Books in the game]]).

::: secret
He's one of [[The Undying]].
:::
