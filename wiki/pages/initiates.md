Every player starts as an **Initiate**: a new [[Emberborn]] who isn't a member of [[The Emberguard]] yet. The Emberguard welcome Initiates kindly, but each is one of hundreds. To be allowed to try for the Emberguard, an Initiate swears the Oath of the Flame, then earns trial marks across the land: Ember, Flame, Blaze and Radiant (see [[Trial marks]]).

When an Initiate has proven themselves, the Emberguard invite them to join. That's the day they learn the truth (see [[Main story]]).
