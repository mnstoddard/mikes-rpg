**Huntmaster Kael** teaches fighting from his lodge by the gate, and leads [[The Wardens]], the rangers who guard the borders against [[The Blight]]. He says the Blight is always worst furthest from a shrine, and the line gets closer every year. He distrusts the Emberguard, but can't say why.
