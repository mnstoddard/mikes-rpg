**Forester Holt** teaches [[Woodcutting]] in the [[Emberhall Training Ground]]. The trees out at the valley's edge are dying, the dead line creeps closer every year, and he's been counting them ([[What Holt Saw]]). He remembers when [[The Thornwood]] still glowed at night.

## In the game (version 0.22)

Holt's lesson opens after [[Smith Brann]]'s. He gives you one bronze axe (his reward no longer adds a spare). Woodcutting is slow by hand and with your first logs, and steady (no lucky streaks): your Mastery of each kind of log matters most, about twice as fast by Mastery 50. Tool hints say what helps: *"Chopping a tree is much faster with a hatchet."*
