**Forester Holt** teaches [[Woodcutting]] in the [[Emberhall Training Ground]]. The trees out at the valley's edge are dying, the dead line creeps closer every year, and he's been counting them ([[What Holt Saw]]). He remembers when [[The Thornwood]] still glowed at night.
