**Miner Greta** teaches [[Mining]] at the practice rocks in the [[Emberhall Training Ground]]. She's found that emberstone veins flow toward [[The shrines]], and that the rock of the [[The crystal cave|Crystal Cave]] is colder than when she was a girl.

## In the game (version 0.22)

Greta's lesson opens after [[Smith Brann]]'s. She gives you one bronze pickaxe at full durability (her reward no longer adds a spare). Mining is steady, like woodcutting: no lucky streaks.
