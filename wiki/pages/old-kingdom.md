**The Old Kingdom** ruled the whole land before [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]]: green from the Greyspine to the sea, ruled from [[Brightcrown]], a city of white stone in what is now [[The Ashen Barrens]]. People remember it as a golden age. [[The Hearthwise]] lived there.

It fell in the [[Sundering War]]. Its ruins are scattered across the Barrens, and its capital is the crater called [[The Sundered Crown]].

Old Kingdom coins dug up in the Barrens show a seed under a crown: the first hint of the [[Heart Seed]].
