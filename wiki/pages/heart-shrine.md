**The Heart Shrine** is the brightest of [[The shrines]], inside [[The Emberhall]]. It's the great shrine of [[Heartvale]], raised by [[Archmage Vale]] right after the Emberfall, over the place the working broke loose. New [[Emberborn]] wake on its steps, and fallen ones come back to it. [[Sister Celene]] tends it.

It looks like a tall flame burning in a wide stone bowl on a stepped base, with embers drifting up from it. Every brazier in the Emberhall is lit from it.

::: secret
It's the deepest pool of stolen ember in the land. Every [[Radiance Ceremony]] pours a champion's ember down its thread to Vale.
:::
