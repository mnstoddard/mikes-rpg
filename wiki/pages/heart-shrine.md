**The Heart Shrine** is the brightest of [[The shrines]], inside [[The Lighthall]]. New [[Emberborn]] wake on its steps, and fallen ones come back to it. [[Sister Celene]] tends it.

::: secret
Every shrine's thread runs here, and from here down to [[The Undying]].
:::
