**King Aldemar XVIII** was the eighteenth and last king of [[Aldmera]], and the leader of [[The Ember Circle]]. He was a mage as well as a king, and the great working to harness [[The Emberheart]] was his plan: to send its warmth into every corner of the kingdom, so no one in Aldmera would go hungry or cold again. He wore the [[Ember Crown]], which let him steer the working.

He died on the night the working broke loose, and his kingdom died with him.

**What everyone is taught:** [[Wick]], his own second, struck him down and seized the working. That was the Emberfall.

::: secret
[[Archmage Vale]] killed him, and twisted the working so its power poured into the Circle instead of the land. Vale took the Ember Crown from his body. The rest of the Circle believe the king died in the burst.
:::

His statue lies buried in [[The Ashen Barrens]], holding a crown with a glowing stone in it (a planned side quest, *Aldemar's Statue*).
