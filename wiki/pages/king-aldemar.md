**King Aldemar XVIII** was the eighteenth and last king of [[Aldmera]], and the leader of [[The Ember Circle]]. He was a mage as well as a king, and the great working to harness [[The Emberheart]] was his plan: to send its warmth into every corner of the kingdom, so no one in Aldmera would go hungry or cold again. He wore the [[Ember Crown]], which let him steer the working.

**What everyone is taught:** when the land's fire woke under the valley, the king went down into it and poured his own ember into the torrent to hold it back. It killed him, and it was only a bandage, but it held for a year: long enough for the Circle to raise the shrines. Nobody is taught about a working. His statue stands beside the Heart Shrine in [[The Emberhall]].

::: secret
The fire woke because of his own working, after [[Archmage Vale]] twisted it so its power poured into the Circle instead of the land. The king's sacrifice was real: he gave his life cleaning up Vale's crime. Vale took the Ember Crown from where he fell. The rest of the Circle believe the working simply failed.
:::

His statue lies buried in [[The Ashen Barrens]], holding a crown with a glowing stone in it (a planned side quest, *Aldemar's Statue*).
