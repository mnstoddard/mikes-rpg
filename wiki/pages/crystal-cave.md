**The Crystal Cave** is a deep cave full of ember crystal, reached through the spider cave past the quarry. Platinum, iridium and gem rocks are mined there.

It's the closest you can get to [[The Heartfire]] on foot: you can hear it beat in the rock, and [[Miner Greta]] says it's slower than when she was a girl. A huge scorched shaft runs from it toward Heartvale, too straight to be natural (a planned quest, *The Old Shaft*).
