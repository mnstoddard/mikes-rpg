**The Thornwood** is the deep forest south of [[Heartvale]], behind the fenced gate. In the game its edge is the danger zone: monsters there attack on sight. It holds the Goblin camp, Spider hollow, Wolf Hollow, an Ashbound camp and [[The Graveyard]].

Its old trees glowed with [[The ember|ember]] at night. Now the woods are going dark: the Dark Forest is spreading toward [[Emberhold]] a few steps a day. [[Forester Holt]] remembers when it still glowed. Its great shrine is run by Warden Crowe (see [[Emberwardens]]); in Act 1 you relight the Thornwood shrine (*[[A Flame in the Thornwood]]*).

::: secret
The shrines pull the glow away, and where it's gone the bark turns black. That's the Dark Forest.
:::
