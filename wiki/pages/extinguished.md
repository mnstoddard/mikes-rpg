**The Extinguished** are the greatest [[Emberborn]] heroes of the past. Each one was honoured with a [[Radiance Ceremony]], where they burned brighter than ever and then went out like a candle. Their statues stand in [[The Emberhall]], where [[Brother Oren]] tells their stories. One plinth stands empty, its name scratched off.

They used to be called the Radiants, and their statues had a room of their own, the Hall of Champions. The next game update moves the statues into the main hall.

## The official story

This is what the Emberguard teach, and what every Hopeful hears on their first day.

- **Ember is precious.** The Heart Shrine can only raise new heroes with ember, and there's only so much to go round.
- **The best heroes give theirs back.** When an Emberborn has done enough, they're invited to a Radiance Ceremony. They lay their hands on the Heart Shrine, blaze up one last time, and let their ember flow back into it. They go out gently, *"like falling asleep by the fire"*.
- **One flame goes out so a hundred can be lit.** Their ember raises the heroes who come after. Every new Emberborn, the Emberguard say, carries a little of the Extinguished inside them.
- **It's a choice, and the highest honour.** Nobody is forced. To be invited is the greatest reward an Emberborn can earn: a statue in the Emberhall, their name in the great ledger, and a pension for any family they left.
- **That's why there's no body.** There's nothing left to bury. They're "resting in the Emberheart", and their families are told to be proud.

The Emberguard's sayings: *"Burn bright, go out kind."* *"One flame goes out so a hundred can be lit."*

## The true story

The Extinguished didn't give their ember back to anyone. They were drained to feed [[The Ember Circle]], who rule the Emberguard as [[The Undying]].

- **Their ember didn't raise new heroes.** It went down the Heart Shrine's thread to the Undying. One Ceremony keeps them alive and strong for years.
- **They were chosen for how much ember they held, not for their deeds.** Every level an Emberborn gains makes their ember stronger. The Emberguard praise and reward the brightest heroes because those are the ones worth draining. "Doing enough" really means "ripe".
- **It wasn't a choice.** The invitation can't be refused. On the day, the champion is given a calming draught brewed for the Emberhall and led down the stair under the Heart Shrine. Few understood what was happening until it was too late.
- **It isn't gentle.** The thread pulls everything out at once. Most are gone in moments. A few fought back, and the Ceremony calendar marks them *"spark flawed"*.
- **Some of them aren't gone.** A few sparks are still trapped in old refined emberstones. The ghosts in [[The Graveyard]] are drained Emberborn still tied to the old shrine.
- **One survived.** [[Sable]], the brightest of them all, was drained in year 460 and lived. Hers is the empty plinth. She founded [[The Ashbound]].
- **Tilda knew.** Tilda Ashwalker found out the truth before her Ceremony. Her last cairn in the Barrens says *"Don't go down the stair."*

Why it matters: every Hopeful is being raised to become one of the Extinguished. The more a player levels up, the brighter they burn, and the closer they are to their own invitation. That's the question at the Joining (see [[Main story]]).
