**The Extinguished** are the greatest [[Emberborn]] heroes of the past. Each one was honoured with a [[Radiance Ceremony]], where they burned brighter than ever and then went out like a candle. Their statues line the walls of the great hall in [[The Emberhall]], where [[Brother Oren]] tells their stories, and small embers drift through the hall in their honour. One plinth near the doors stands empty: [[Wick]]'s.

Every one of the Extinguished went through a Radiance Ceremony: that's what makes someone one of them. None of them died any other way.

They used to be called the Radiants, and their statues had a room of their own, the Hall of Champions. Since version 0.14 the statues line the walls of the Emberhall's great hall instead, and the game calls them the Extinguished too.

## The official story

This is what the Emberguard teach, and what every Initiate hears on their first day.

- **Every Emberborn fades.** After a few hundred years, every fall and every rebirth has worn them thin (see [[The Fading]]). Everyone has watched an old champion grow grey and forgetful.
- **The Ceremony is rest.** When a champion has carried the flame long enough, the Emberguard invite them to lay it down. They lay their hands on the Heart Shrine, blaze up one last time, and go out gently, *"like falling asleep by the fire"*, with their friends watching.
- **One flame lights many.** Their ember doesn't go to waste: the Heart Shrine uses it to raise several new heroes. Every new Emberborn, the Emberguard say, carries a little of the Extinguished inside them.
- **It's a choice, and the highest honour.** Most champions ask for it themselves when they feel ready. To be found worthy means a statue in the Emberhall, their name in the great ledger, and an ember in the hall that never goes out.
- **Refusing is shameful.** It's allowed, but it means saying "I'm not done yet" while everyone you love says it's your time. Almost no one ever has. The one everyone knows about is [[Sable]].

The Emberguard's sayings: *"Burn bright, go out kind."* *"One flame goes out so many can be lit."*

## The true story

The Extinguished chose the Ceremony, believed in it, and went in peace. That's what makes it so cruel.

- **Their ember didn't raise new heroes.** It went down the Heart Shrine's thread to [[Archmage Vale]]. Vale created the Ceremony around year 200 for exactly this. One Ceremony feeds him for years.
- **The Emberguard decide who's "ready", and speed it up.** Blessings, Tithes and praise are tuned to wear the brightest down faster, so they feel the Fading early. The dim and ordinary are never invited. "Ready" really means "ripe".
- **It isn't gentle.** The thread pulls everything out at once. A few champions fought it at the last moment, and the Ceremony calendar marks them *"spark flawed"*.
- **The floating embers aren't tributes.** They're scraps of sparks the Ceremony couldn't fully drain, still drifting in the hall. Some sparks are trapped in old refined emberstones too, and the ghosts in [[The Graveyard]] are the oldest of them.
- **Tilda knew.** Tilda Ashwalker found out the truth before her Ceremony. She went anyway, because she believed nobody would listen, but her last cairn in the Barrens says *"Don't go down the stair."*

Why it matters: every Initiate is being raised to become one of the Extinguished. The more a player levels up, the brighter they burn, and the closer they are to their own invitation. That's what the player realises at their Joining (see [[Main story]]).
