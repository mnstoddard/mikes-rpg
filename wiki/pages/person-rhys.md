**Rhys** is an [[Emberborn]] who woke just before you, and your friendly rival, always one step ahead. You meet him at the end of Act 1, when you light the shrine at the valley's edge. He lost the emberstone he woke up holding.

::: secret
In Act 3, after four great-shrine trials, Rhys is invited to join first. At his Joining he goes down the Emberhall stair and comes back up drained, grey, and back to level 1. "His spark was flawed." In truth he was shown the truth and said no. He joins [[The Ashbound]].
:::
