**Rhys** is an [[Emberborn]] who woke just before you, and your friendly rival, always one trial ahead. He lost the emberstone he woke up holding.

::: secret
In Act 3 Rhys is invited to join first. At his Joining he goes down the Lighthall stair and comes back up drained, grey, and back to level 1. "His spark was flawed." In truth he was shown the truth and said no. He joins [[The Ashbound]].
:::
