**Brother Oren** gives tours of the statues in the great hall of [[The Emberhall]]: [[King Aldemar XVIII]], [[Archmage Vale]] and the rest of [[The Ember Circle]], and the empty plinth that was [[Wick]]'s. He points new Emberborn out to the garden, where [[The Extinguished]] stand. The tour is the second step of the Prologue's *The Hall of Embers*. He tells the Emberguard's story of the [[Sundering War]]. Honest.
