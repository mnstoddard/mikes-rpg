**Keeper Oren** gives tours of the statues in the great hall of [[The Emberhall]]: [[King Aldemar XVIII]], [[Archmage Vale]] and the rest of [[The Ember Circle]], and the empty plinth that was [[Wick]]'s. He points new Emberborn out to the garden, where [[The Extinguished]] stand. The tour is the second step of the Prologue's *The Hall of Embers*. He walks you from statue to statue himself, telling the Emberguard's story of the Emberfall and the [[Sundering War]].

::: secret
Like every Keeper, he's in on the Emberguard's secret (version 0.22). He tells the official story knowing it's a lie.
:::
