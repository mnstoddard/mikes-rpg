**The shrines** stand all across the land, kept lit by [[The Emberguard]]. New [[Emberborn]] wake at them, and fallen Emberborn come back at the nearest one. A dark shrine raises no one. Every child is taught that the shrines saved the land after the [[Sundering War]], and that they keep [[The Blight]] back.

The brightest is [[The Heart Shrine]] in [[The Lighthall]]. Out in [[Heartvale]] there's the Westroad shrine and the Thornwood shrine, both relit in Act 1. Each region has a great shrine run by an [[Emberwardens|Emberwarden]].

::: secret
Each shrine is a tap. It quietly draws [[The ember|ember]] out of the land around it and sends it along an invisible thread to the Heart Shrine, and from there down to [[The Undying]]. That stolen ember keeps them alive. The shrines don't keep the Blight back: **they cause it**.

The shrines were meant to close when the war was won. The Drowned Shrine off [[Saltmere Coast]] sank with its carving: *"The shrines shall close when the war is won."*

The shrines also shape stolen ember into bodies: that's what Emberborn are. Each time an Emberborn falls and comes back, the shrine skims a little ember off them ([[The Tithe]]).
:::
