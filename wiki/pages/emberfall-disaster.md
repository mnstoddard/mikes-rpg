**The Emberfall** was the disaster that began the calendar (years are counted *After the Fall*). The land's fire burst up out of the earth under [[Heartvale]] in a pillar of flame, and burning embers rained down across the [[Old Kingdom]] for a whole year. The ground around it fell in, leaving the round valley that is Heartvale today. Embers that landed in rock cooled into glowing shards worth more than gold, and the lords' fight over them became the [[Sundering War]].

## What people believe

*"The land's fire burst in anger. No one was to blame."* That's what every child is taught, and what the Emberguard's books say (see [[Books in the game]]).

::: secret
The Emberfall wasn't natural. [[The Ember Circle]], the greatest mages of the Old Kingdom led by [[Archmage Vale]], dug a great shaft down to [[The Emberheart]] and cast a spell to drink from it directly, so they could live forever. The fire burst up out of their shaft. The shaft is still there, under [[The Emberhall]].
:::
