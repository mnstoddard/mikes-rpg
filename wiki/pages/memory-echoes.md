title: Memory Echoes
category: Story
icon: 🧩

---
**Memory Echoes** (🧩) are short glimpses of the past that some quests show you, so players can see history for themselves instead of being told. Planned echoes include the Circle in a ring around a pit of fire on the night of the Emberfall, and one of them stepping toward the king (*The Old Shaft*), and Halric the Dawnbringer's first day, around year 30, with a young, smiling Vale (*The First One*).
