**Memory Echoes** (🧩) are short glimpses of the past that some quests show you, so players can see history for themselves instead of being told. Planned echoes include robed mages chanting around a pit of fire (*The Old Shaft*), and [[Old Maren]]'s first day in year 40, with a young, smiling Vale (*Maren's First Day*).
