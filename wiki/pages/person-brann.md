**Smith Brann** teaches [[Smelting]] and [[Smithing]] in the [[Emberhall Training Ground]], and has his smithy on Forge Row. Gruff and honest. His forge coal has burned for 800 years.

::: secret
The coal is an Emberfall shard that has never gone out: ember lasts if you don't take too much. Later in the story he forges your Severing Blade.
:::

## His lesson (version 0.22, Mike's note)

Brann teaches first, before [[Forester Holt]] and [[Miner Greta]], so you can always make yourself a new tool. He hands you a bronze bar and a log, and at Smithing level 1 **the only thing you can make is the hammer**. Then he gives you copper and tin to smelt three bars, and by then your Smithing has gone up enough to make **tongs**. He doesn't hand out tools: you make your own.

Every Smithing level unlocks one more thing at the anvil, in this order (for bronze; better metals need more levels on top):
