The six **Emberwardens** each keep a region's great shrine, and each gives the player a quest chain in Acts 2 and 3, ending in a trial (see [[Main story]]). In Act 4 the player hunts all six down. Each carries the name of the [[The Ember Circle|Ember Circle]] member who first raised that shrine. Everyone believes the names are titles, handed down from warden to warden since the Circle passed into legend.

[[The Heart Shrine]] in Heartvale is kept by the Emberguard's High Keeper. [[Frostfell]]'s shrine is frozen, and has no warden.

::: secret
All six are [[The Undying]]. Four are the original Circle members who sided with [[Archmage Vale]] in the [[Sundering War]]. The other two were chosen by Vale from the most corrupt of the Emberguard and Emberborn to take the dead members' names and shrines. Nobody outside the Undying knows.

They collect anything with ember in it: every glowing fish was taken from Warden Merrow's feast before cooking, and Warden Flint's emberstone carts to the Emberhall don't add up.
:::

None of them are in the game yet.
