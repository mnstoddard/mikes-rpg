[[Heartvale]] sits in the middle, with [[Emberhold]] and [[The Heart Shrine]] on its western edge, and [[The Hollow Heart]] deep below it. Around it:

- **North:** [[Greyspine Peaks]], and beyond them [[Frostfell]] with Wick's frozen shrine.
- **East:** [[Saltmere Coast]] and the sea.
- **South:** [[The Thornwood]], with [[The Tangle]] to the south-east and [[Blackfen]] to the south-west.
- **West:** [[The Ashen Barrens]], and past their far edge [[The Sundered Crown]], the ruins of Whitestone.
- **Underneath:** [[The Deeps]], caves that run under every region.

Each gold dot is a great shrine with a city around it. This is a sketch of where things are, not the final game map. Only Heartvale is in the game so far.

## Travelling between regions

The land between the cities is where [[The Withering]] is worst, so nobody wanders it lightly. There are three ways to get around.

### 1. The King's Roads

[[Aldmera]] built roads everywhere, and the main ones still run from Emberhold out to every great shrine city. The Emberguard keep **small shrines** all along them, a few miles apart, so the roads stay warm and fairly safe, and an Emberborn who falls on the road wakes at the nearest one. Off the road, the wilds get dangerous fast.

The first time you go to a region (its *Arrival* stage, in Act 2), you walk or ride its King's Road. The journey is part of the quest: escorting a caravan, relighting a roadside shrine that's gone dark, clearing an ambush.

::: secret
The roads are safe for the same reason the land beside them is dying: every roadside shrine pulls ember out of the wilds around it to keep the road warm. That's why the Withering is worst just off the King's Roads.
:::

### 2. Shrine-walking

Once you've reached a great shrine and it knows you, you can **shrine-walk**: step into one great shrine and out of another, along the threads that link them. It's the game's fast travel. The Emberguard teach it as a gift of the Heart Shrine to its heroes.

::: secret
You're travelling along the same threads that carry stolen ember to the Undying. In Act 4, after Wick cuts your thread, shrine-walking stops working until you've turned a great shrine. You can then walk between the shrines you've turned, and Ashbound hearths.
:::

### 3. Boats and caravans

- A **ferry** runs from Saltmere's harbour down the coast to [[The Tangle]].
- **Caravans** cross the Barrens on the Whitestone road, as far as it's safe.
- The roads to [[Frostfell]] and [[The Sundered Crown]] are closed, by snow and by the Emberguard, until the story opens them.

### Story gating

All six outer regions open at the end of Act 1 (see [[Main story]]). Frostfell opens in Act 4, the Hollow Heart at the end of Act 4, and the Sundered Crown in the endgame.
