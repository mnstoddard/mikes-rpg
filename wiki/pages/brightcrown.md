**Brightcrown** was the capital of the [[Old Kingdom]], a city of white stone in the west. The [[Ember Crown]]'s blast tore it apart in the [[Sundering War]], leaving the crater called [[The Sundered Crown]]. A broken milestone on the King's Road in the Barrens still reads *"Brightcrown, 40 miles."*
