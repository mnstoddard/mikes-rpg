**The Oath of the Flame** is sworn at [[The Heart Shrine]] at the end of the Prologue. The Emberguard teach that it lets the shrine know you, so it can always bring you home. Swearing it makes an [[Initiates|Initiate]] an Ember.

> "I am Emberborn. I carry the flame. Where I fall, the Emberheart will raise me."

::: secret
## What the Oath really is

The Oath is a spell. Every word of it is a binding, written by [[Archmage Vale]] long ago. It ties the Emberborn who swears it to the Heart Shrine with a thread of ember, and through the thread to [[The Ember Circle]].

- **It starts the Tithe.** Before the Oath, a fall costs nothing. After it, every fall pays [[The Tithe]], and a share of everything you learn flows down the thread.
- **It feeds the Circle.** What flows down the thread goes to [[The Undying]], most of it to Vale. The brighter an Emberborn burns, the more the thread carries. That's why the Emberguard push everyone to build their flame.
- **It's what a Ceremony pulls on.** The [[Radiance Ceremony]] drains a champion all at once, down the same thread.
- **The clue is in the quest.** When you swear it, you feel "a tug deep in your chest, like a thread pulled tight".
- **Wick can break it.** In Act 4, Wick breaks the player's Oath. The Tithe ends, and grey ash marks start to creep up their hands (see [[Main story]]).
