**The Undying** are the leaders of [[The Emberguard]]: the original [[The Ember Circle|Ember Circle]], who caused [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]] 500 years ago and have lived on stolen ember ever since. They live below [[The Lighthall]], down its sealed stair, in the Undying's Sanctum.

Known members: [[Archmage Vale]] (as [[Enchanter Vale]] and the hooded High Keeper), [[Keeper Aldric]], and the four [[Emberwardens]].

Emberguard players who choose to join become Undying at the end of Act 4B.
