**The Undying** are the secret leaders of [[The Emberguard]]: what's left of [[The Ember Circle]] after the [[Sundering War]], and the people Vale has raised to join them. They've lived on the ember their shrines pull in for eight hundred years. They meet below [[The Emberhall]], down its sealed stair, in the Undying's Sanctum.

Members:

- [[Archmage Vale]], as [[Enchanter Vale]] and the hooded High Keeper. The strongest by far, fed by every [[Radiance Ceremony]].
- Crowe, Flint, Merrow and Dunn: the Circle members who sided with Vale. Today they're four of the [[Emberwardens]].
- Warden Ivy and Warden Reed: impostors wearing the names of the members killed on Wick's side.
- [[Keeper Aldric]] and a few others Vale rewarded after the war.

Only Vale knows he killed [[King Aldemar XVIII]]. The others believe the Emberfall was an accident, and that the land would fall apart without them. They tell themselves the shrines are the only thing holding the torrent back.

Emberguard players who choose to join become Undying at the end of Act 4B.
