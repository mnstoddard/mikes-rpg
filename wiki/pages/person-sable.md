**Sable** leads [[The Ashbound]]. Her hands are grey with ash. To the player at their Joining, she offers nothing but the truth: *"Better ash than a thief."*

## What the Emberguard say about her

This story is new. Until a few years ago, Sable was one of [[The Extinguished]], honoured like the rest, and nobody in [[Emberhold]] had a bad word to say about her. Today every Hopeful hears this version:

- Long ago, Sable was the brightest champion the shrines ever raised, and she was honoured with a [[Radiance Ceremony]].
- **The Heart Shrine refused her.** Her spark was flawed. Instead of going out gently like the others, she came back, wrong. The Emberguard call it the greatest shame an Emberborn can carry: the land itself wouldn't take her ember.
- **Her shame twisted her.** She went bitter and mad, and now she leads the Ashbound, smashing the shrines that honoured her.
- **She's killing the land.** The Withering creeping out of the west, the dying trees, the grey ground: the Emberguard say that's the Ashbound's doing, poisoning the land round the shrines to spite them.

People say her name the way they'd spit. Brother Oren calls her "every family's black sheep".

## The empty plinth

The plinth near the Emberhall doors is the only one with no statue. Someone has scratched the name off the stone, very carefully, letter by letter. Only the first letter is left under the dust: **S**.

For more than three hundred years, Sable's statue stood there among the Extinguished, and Oren's tour stopped at it with pride. When the Emberguard found out who leads the Ashbound, they pulled her statue down in a single night and scratched her name away. Now Oren hurries Hopefuls past it, and it has become a warning: *this is what happens to a spark that isn't worthy.*

## What really happened

**She went down to the Emberheart.** Before her Ceremony, Sable did something no Emberborn was ever allowed to do. She went down past the shrines, through [[The Hollow Heart]], all the way to [[The Emberheart]] itself, and touched it. A piece of it took hold in her, much as it did in [[The Ember Circle]] when they drank from it in year 0. That piece wasn't stolen ember. It was the land's own fire, and it was hers now.

**The Ceremony couldn't drain it.** Around year 140 she was honoured with her Ceremony like every champion before her, and the thread pulled out everything it could reach. It couldn't take the piece of the Emberheart. When the Ceremony was over, the Emberguard believed she was gone like all the rest. They gave her a statue, and the Ceremony calendar says only *"spark flawed"*.

**She came back as a new Emberborn.** That spark of the Emberheart was enough for a shrine to raise her again. She woke on the steps of a far-off shrine like any new Emberborn: no past, no name, no memory, holding a pebble of emberstone. Nobody knew who she was, and neither did she.

**Three hundred years of remembering.** Sable lived as an ordinary Emberborn under other names, keeping away from the Emberhall. But the piece of the Emberheart kept something the shrine hadn't: her memories came back slowly, in pieces, mostly in dreams. She could feel the Emberheart's heartbeat in her chest, and over the centuries she felt it growing weaker. Wherever she found a shrine, she felt the pull that was draining it.

**The truth, and the Ashbound.** Around year 470 she finally remembered all of it: who she had been, what the Ceremony really was, and what she had felt the thread do. The shrines are a siphon, the Ceremonies are a harvest, and she had been food for [[The Undying]]. She cut her own thread, as [[Old Maren]] had, gathered the few who'd believe her, Emberborn and [[The Rooted]] alike, and founded the Ashbound. Her hands have been grey with ash ever since.

**Found out (about year 495).** For twenty years the Emberguard thought the Ashbound were just bandits. Then a Keeper saw their leader up close and knew her face from the statue. The Emberguard tore the statue down, and started the story of the flawed spark the land refused.

**The lies are the cover-up.** The Withering began in the Barrens centuries before the Ashbound existed (Tilda Ashwalker fought it while Sable was still a statue). The Blight is the shrines draining the land dry. Blaming the Ashbound hides that, and turns Sable's name into a warning for every Hopeful who might wonder what a Ceremony really is.

**What she is now.** Sable is the only one of the Extinguished who ever came back, and the only person besides the Circle who has touched the Emberheart. That makes her the most dangerous person in Embervale to the Undying. Her fire is tied to the land's: if the Emberheart dies, she dies with it, which is why she'll burn every shrine to save it.
