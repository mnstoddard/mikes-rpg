**Sable** leads [[The Ashbound]]. Her hands are grey with ash. She's the one who takes the player to Wick after Rhys is drained, and who gets them out of the Emberhall after they refuse Vale.

## What the Emberguard say about her

- About twenty years ago, Sable was the brightest champion the shrines had raised in a century, and she was chosen for a [[Radiance Ceremony]]: the highest honour there is.
- **She ran.** The night before her Ceremony, she vanished. Her friends had gathered and the Emberhall was decorated for her. She simply wasn't there in the morning.
- **Her shame drove her mad.** She fled her honour, and couldn't live with it. Now she leads bandits in the woods, smashing the shrines that raised her.
- **She's killing the land.** The Withering, the dying trees, the grey ground: the Emberguard say that's the Ashbound's doing.

People say her name the way they'd spit. Refusing a Ceremony is the most shameful thing an Emberborn can do, and Sable is the only one most people have ever heard of. Keeper Oren calls her "every family's black sheep".

## What really happened

**Wick found her first.** Sable had no doubts. She was proud to be chosen, and ready. A few weeks before her Ceremony, a stranger with an old, tired face found her alone and told her what a Ceremony really does. She didn't believe him. He showed her: a ledger page, a trapped spark in an old emberstone, and the Fading the Emberguard had been speeding up in her.

**The night before:** she went to the Emberhall that night to see for herself, and found the stair under the Heart Shrine. What she saw down there was enough. She walked out of Emberhold before dawn and never came back.

**The Ashbound:** the stranger was [[Wick]]. Together they started the Ashbound. She's its face and its leader; he stays hidden, researching. With his help she cut her own thread so the shrines could never pull her back in, and since then ash has been creeping up her hands. She knows what that means, and she chose it.

**The lies are the cover-up.** The Withering began centuries before the Ashbound existed. Blaming them hides what Vale is taking from the shrines, and turns Sable's name into a warning for every champion who might wonder what a Ceremony really is.
