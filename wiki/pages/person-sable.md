**Sable** leads [[The Ashbound]]. People in town say she went mad. Her hands are grey with ash. To the player at their Joining, she offers nothing but the truth: *"Better ash than a thief."*

::: secret
Sable was once the Emberguard's brightest champion, and was meant to be one of [[The Extinguished]]. At her [[Radiance Ceremony]] in year 460 she was drained, and survived: the only one who ever has. She founded the Ashbound. The empty plinth in the Emberhall, with the scratched-off name, was hers.
:::
