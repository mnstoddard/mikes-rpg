**Sable** leads [[The Ashbound]]. Her hands are grey with ash. To the player at their Joining, she offers nothing but the truth: *"Better ash than a thief."*

## What the Emberguard say about her

This story is new. Until a few years ago, nobody in [[Emberhold]] had a bad word to say about Sable. Today every Hopeful hears this version:

- Long ago, Sable was the brightest champion the shrines ever raised, and she was invited to a [[Radiance Ceremony]], the highest honour there is.
- **She ran from it.** The night before her Ceremony she fled, a coward who wouldn't give her ember back for the heroes who'd come after her.
- **Her shame twisted her.** She went bitter and mad, and now she leads the Ashbound, smashing the shrines she was meant to honour.
- **She's killing the land.** The Withering creeping out of the west, the dying trees, the grey ground: the Emberguard say that's the Ashbound's doing, poisoning the land round the shrines to spite them.

People say her name the way they'd spit. Brother Oren calls her "every family's black sheep".

## The empty plinth

The plinth near the Emberhall doors is the only one with no statue. Someone has scratched the name off the stone, very carefully, letter by letter. Only the first letter is left under the dust: **S**.

For more than three hundred years, Sable's statue stood there among [[The Extinguished]], and Oren's tour stopped at it with pride. When the Emberguard found out who leads the Ashbound, they pulled her statue down in a single night and scratched her name away. They couldn't explain the empty plinth, so now Oren hurries Hopefuls past it, and it has become a warning: *this is what happens to those who refuse the honour.*

## What really happened

**She went down to the Emberheart.** Around year 140, Sable was chosen for a Radiance Ceremony. Something about it frightened her. The night before, she went somewhere no Emberborn was ever allowed to go: down past the shrines, through [[The Hollow Heart]], all the way to [[The Emberheart]] itself.

**It took her in.** She touched the Emberheart, and a piece of it took hold in her, much as it did in [[The Ember Circle]] when they drank from it in year 0. She doesn't need the shrines or the siphon to live. Her ember comes straight from the land's heart. It's why she has hardly aged in three hundred and sixty years.

**It took her memory.** The Emberheart burned away everything she had been. She climbed out of the Hollow Heart with grey, ash-burned hands, and no idea who she was, what the Emberguard were, or why she'd gone down there.

**The Emberguard made the best of it.** Their champion had vanished on the night before her Ceremony, and they couldn't admit it. So they told everyone she had gone into the Emberheart with honour, counted her among the Extinguished, and put up her statue. The Ceremony calendar says only *"spark flawed"*.

**Three hundred years of remembering.** Sable wandered the land under other names, never staying anywhere long, because people notice a woman who doesn't grow old. Her memories came back slowly, in pieces, mostly in dreams. The one thing she never lost was the Emberheart's heartbeat. She could feel it in her chest, and over the centuries she felt it growing weaker. Wherever she found a shrine, she felt the pull that was draining it.

**The truth, and the Ashbound.** Around year 470 she finally put it together: the shrines are a siphon, the Ceremonies are a harvest, and she had been meant to be food for [[The Undying]]. She gathered the few who'd believe her, Emberborn and [[The Rooted]] alike, and founded the Ashbound.

**Found out (about year 495).** For twenty years the Emberguard thought the Ashbound were just bandits. Then a Keeper saw their leader up close and knew her face from the statue. The Emberguard tore the statue down, and started the story of the coward who fled her Ceremony.

**The lies are the cover-up.** The Withering began in the Barrens centuries before the Ashbound existed (Tilda Ashwalker fought it long before Sable remembered her own name). The Blight is the shrines draining the land dry. Blaming the Ashbound hides that, and turns Sable's name into a warning for every Hopeful who might wonder what a Ceremony really is.

**What she is now.** Sable is the only person alive who has touched the Emberheart and come back, besides the Circle. That makes her the most dangerous person in Embervale to the Undying, and she knows the Emberheart better than anyone. If it dies, she dies with it. Her fire is tied to the land's, which is why she'll burn every shrine to save it.
