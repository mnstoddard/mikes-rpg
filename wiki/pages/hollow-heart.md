**The Hollow Heart** is a cavern deep under the land, and the end of the main story. It's where [[The Emberheart]] burns: a lake of living fire with thousands of thin threads rising from it, one for every [[Emberborn]].

In the planned quest *The Heartbeat*, you put your hand in the flame and feel the land's heart beat, slow and tired.
