**The Hollow Heart** is the endgame area: a vast cavern deep under [[Heartvale]], reached from the bottom of the old shaft under [[The Emberhall]]. There's no shrine here, and nothing to bring you back but your own strength. It's where [[The Emberheart]] burns: a lake of living fire with thousands of thin threads rising from it, one for every [[Emberborn]].

It's where [[King Aldemar XVIII]] died, and where the Circle's working broke loose. In the planned quest *The Living Fire*, you put your hand in the flame and feel how much the land has lost.

::: secret
Half of the [[Ember Crown]] lies down here, where the king fell. In the endgame the Emberguard come for it, and the Ashbound come to plant the [[Ember Seed]].
:::
