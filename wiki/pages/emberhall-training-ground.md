The **Emberhall Training Ground** is a hedged yard just east of [[The Emberhall]], in [[Emberhold]]. [[The Emberguard]] pay every trainer in the city to teach the new [[Emberborn]] here, so every lesson is a gift from the Emberhall.

It was added in version 0.17 to make the Prologue shorter and take every player through every skill in one place.

## What's there

- A pond (minnows by hand, trout with a rod): [[Old Maren]] teaches Fishing and Cooking at the stove beside it.
- A grove of practice pines and oaks: [[Forester Holt]] (Woodcutting).
- Practice rocks (copper, tin, iron and coal): [[Miner Greta]] (Mining).
- A furnace and anvil: [[Smith Brann]] (Smelting and Smithing).
- A workbench, spinning wheel and tanning rack: [[Woodworker Rook]] (Handicraft).
- Dig patches: [[Herbalist Wren]] (Foraging).
- An alchemy table: [[Alchemist Moss]] (Alchemy).
- An enchanting altar: [[Archmage Rowan]] (Magic and Enchanting).
- A chicken coop and a rat pit: [[Huntmaster Kael]] (fighting and Hunting).
- Two farm plots: [[Farmer Odo]] (Farming).

## In the Prologue

After "Light the Way", Keeper Aldric sends you here ("The Training Ground" chapter). Old Maren's lessons come first; then the other trainers open up, one skill leading to the next (Brann after Greta, Rook after Holt, Moss after Wren, Rowan after Kael). The Oath of the Flame opens once you've learned from every trainer.

## Later: skill challenges (planned)

Once you've sworn the Oath, the trainers can also be found in the Emberhall, where they'll give **skill challenge quests**: harder jobs in their skill that give bonus XP in it. Some need you to level up other skills first. See [[Side quests]].
