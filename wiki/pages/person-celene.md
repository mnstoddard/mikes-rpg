**Keeper Celene** is the healer at [[The Heart Shrine]], and the first face every new [[Emberborn]] sees ([[Awakening]]). She's Emberborn herself: she woke on the same steps long ago, so she talks about "us" when you wake.

::: secret
She's a Keeper, so she's in on the Emberguard's secret (version 0.22). She means every kind word she says to you, and she still sends every Emberborn she wakes toward the Radiance Ceremony.
:::
