**The Ember Crown** was the crown of [[King Aldemar XVIII]], set with a stone of pure ember. It was the focus of [[The Ember Circle]]'s great working: wearing it, the king could steer the power of [[The Emberheart]]. It was lost on the night of [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]], and is said to have shattered with the king.

::: secret
[[Archmage Vale]] took it from the king's body. The Crown cracked in two in the burst. One half is set in Vale's staff (he never lets anyone touch it). The other half fell into [[The Hollow Heart]], where the king died, and lies there still. Whole, it would let its wearer command the Emberheart itself, and that's what the Emberguard search for in the endgame.
:::
