**The Ember Crown** was forged by [[King Aldemar]] from the biggest shard of [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]]. He used it as a weapon in the [[Sundering War]], and its blast destroyed [[Brightcrown]].

::: secret
The Crown is in two halves. One half lies in the Crown Vault under [[The Sundered Crown]]. The other is set in [[Archmage Vale]]'s staff (he never lets anyone touch it). Whole, it would let [[The Undying]] command [[The Emberheart]] itself. It's what the Emberguard search for in the endgame.
:::
