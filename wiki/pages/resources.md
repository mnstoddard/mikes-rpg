[[Heartvale]] has the starter tier of every gathering skill, so new players can train everything at home. Each other region is the only place to find the higher tiers for one or two skills. Crafting skills (Smithing, Cooking, Alchemy, Enchanting, Handicraft, Jewelry) can be trained anywhere, but their best materials come from these regions, which gives players a reason to travel.

| Region | Main skill | Only found here |
|---|---|---|
| [[Heartvale]] (1 to 20) | Every skill, starter tier. Best home for Farming | Pine, oak and birch trees. Copper and tin. Minnow, trout and perch. Basic forage. Your farm plot |
| [[The Thornwood]] (forest) | Woodcutting | Maple (level 20), yew (35), and elderwood (70) deep in the old wood. Truffles and night mushrooms |
| [[Greyspine Peaks]] (volcanic) | Mining | Iron (20), coal (30), silver (40), emberstone veins (25), and rubies (50) in the lava rock |
| [[The Tangle]] (jungle) | Foraging and Hunting | Ironwood trees (50), rare jungle herbs for top Alchemy, emeralds (30), big game hides |
| [[Saltmere Coast]] | Fishing | Tuna (45) and swordfish (60), and pike (30) in the river mouths |
| [[The Ashen Barrens]] (desert) | Mining (treasure) | Gold (55), topaz (20), and Aldmeran relics dug from the sand, for Trading or Enchanting |
| [[Blackfen]] (swamp) | Alchemy ingredients | Eels (75), and swamp and night herbs for the strongest potions |
| [[Frostfell]] (tundra) | Mining and Hunting | Mithril (70), sapphires (40), and thick furs for the best leather armor |
| [[The Deeps]] (caves) | Mining (gems) | Platinum (75), amethyst (10), and gem rocks of every kind as dungeon rewards |
| [[The Hollow Heart]] | Mining | Iridium (85), the rarest ore |
| [[The Sundered Crown]] (endgame) | | Ancient Aldmeran seeds for top Farming crops, and royal treasure |

## Differences from the game today

- In the game, the higher ores, gems and trees can all be found around Heartvale: the [[The crystal cave|Crystal Cave]] has platinum, iridium and gem rocks. Following this plan means moving them out in a future update, or treating the Crystal Cave as the first of the Deeps.
- Jungle herbs, swamp herbs, furs and Aldmeran relics don't exist in the game yet.
