**Orla the Kind** was one of [[The Extinguished]]. Through the Grey Fever of year 760 she went from door to door nursing the sick. The fever took her nine times, and nine times the shrine sent her back to keep working. When the fever had passed, in year 761, the Emberguard honoured her with a [[Radiance Ceremony]]. People remember her as a saint. In the planned quest *Lanterns for the Dead*, the lantern at her statue keeps going out.

::: secret
Orla's spark isn't resting: it's trapped in an old refined emberstone. In the planned quest *Orla's Rest*, you use [[Ember trap]] to free it and carry it to [[The Graveyard]], and her lantern finally stays lit.
:::
