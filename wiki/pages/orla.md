**Orla the Kind** was one of [[The Extinguished]]. She died of the Grey Fever in year 460 after rising nine times, and people remember her as a saint. In the planned quest *Lanterns for the Dead*, the lantern at her statue keeps going out.

::: secret
Orla's spark isn't resting: it's trapped in an old refined emberstone. In the planned quest *Orla's Rest*, you use [[Ember trap]] to free it and carry it to [[The Graveyard]], and her lantern finally stays lit.
:::
