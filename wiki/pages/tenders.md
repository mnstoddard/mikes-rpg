**The Tenders** were the country folk of [[Aldmera]] who lived by the one rule of [[The ember]]: *"Take only what the land gives back."* They tended the land like a hearth: a little fuel in, a little warmth out, never more. Their saying is still painted over a few old doorways.

Their ways live on in odd places. The goblins of [[Greyspine Peaks]] sing a Tender song to keep the grey sickness away, and [[The Ashbound]] build hearths that burn without a shrine, the Tender way.

They used to be called the Hearthwise in the wiki.
