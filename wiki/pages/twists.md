| 1 | The Withering isn't bad luck, and the shrines aren't failing. Something is drinking from them. | Holt's count: trees near shrines are fine, trees at the edge are dying, and the edge is moving in; Odo's blessings fade faster every year; [[Herbalist Wren]]'s maps show a tightening ring; a relit shrine's warmth is pulled away down a thread. | Act 1 (hinted), Act 3 (proved) |
| 2 | The [[Radiance Ceremony]] doesn't light new heroes. It drains champions into Vale. | Old champions "feel the Fading" suspiciously early; "spark flawed" in the Ceremony calendar; Tilda's cairn: "Don't go down the stair." | Act 2 (hinted), end of Act 3 |
| 3 | The Emberguard's leaders are the old [[The Ember Circle|Ember Circle]], alive as [[The Undying]]. | "THE FLAME EATS" painted on a wall; the Tithe mark; Emberwardens who carry the same names as the Circle. | End of Act 3, from Wick |
| 4 | Two of the [[Emberwardens]], Ivy and Reed, are impostors. The real ones died on Wick's side. | Warden Ivy doesn't know an old Tangle song every child there knows; the Blackfen shrine's carving uses a name Warden Reed doesn't answer to. | Act 3 (hinted), Act 4 |
| 5 | [[Wick]] never tried to tear the shrines down, and he's behind [[The Ashbound]]. | The unscratched names on the frozen shrine; an old account of the working that never blames him; Rook's note "The W was never his crime." | End of Act 3 |
| 6 | [[High Keeper Aldric]] is [[Archmage Vale]]. | Aldric has welcomed Initiates for as long as anyone remembers; no one knows where Vale is buried; Aldric wrote *On the Shrines* as if he'd been there. | The Joining, end of Act 3 |
| 7 | The Emberfall wasn't the land's fire waking on its own. The Circle's working caused it, and Vale broke it on purpose. The king died cleaning up Vale's crime. | The page cut from the library's oldest book; the *Old Shaft* Memory Echo; half a crown in Vale's staff. | Endgame |
| 8 | [[The Emberheart]] is losing its warmth, and the land far from the shrines is going cold. | The Crystal Cave's rock is colder than it was; Frostfell's ice moves south. | Act 4 |
| 9 | The [[Ember Seed]] could heal the land, and let Emberborn live without the shrines. | Odo's old seed jar; Aldmeran coins show a seed. | Endgame |
| 10 | The [[Ember Crown]] is in two halves; one is in Vale's staff. | Vale never lets anyone touch his staff. | End of Act 4 |
