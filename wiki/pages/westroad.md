**The Westroad** is the road west out of [[Emberhold]], with a roadside shrine. Ashbound scouts watch it. In Act 1 you rebuild its shrine (*[[Rebuild the Shrine]]*), and find *[[Ash on the Road]]*. The road on toward the Barrens is closed for now.

[[The Ashbound]] smash its shrine first, because it's closest to Emberhold.
