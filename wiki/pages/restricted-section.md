**The restricted section** is the whole west-south wing of [[The Emberhall]]: what used to be the stores and the ledger room, made into one locked area bigger than the library, with tall shelves and glass cases chained shut (a second floor and a spiral stair are planned). [[Brother Tomas]] moved out into the great hall to make room. Its door off the great hall is barred. From the library you can see into it through a tall iron grille, but the gate is locked, and only the Keepers have the key.

## Trying the gate

From the very first day, a player can walk up to the grille. When they get there, [[Sister Lisette]] hurries over with a reason why it's closed today. She picks a different excuse each time:

> Oh, not in there, dear! The roof's leaking again. The old books are drying out, and the Keepers don't want a single page touched until they're dry.

> Ah! Mind the gate. Woodworm. A whole shelf of it. We're smoking them out, and you'd cough for a week.

> That's the Keepers' reading room. They're copying out the old records, and it all has to stay in order. Very dull, honestly.

> The stair's broken. Brother Tomas has been meaning to fix it for, oh, a year now.

> Not today. Maybe when you're a Flame. Or a Blaze. Keeper Aldric decides these things, not me.

If the player keeps trying (three times in a minute or so), she gets a little flustered: *"Please. I'd be in terrible trouble."*

## The quest (planned, Act 3)

*The Restricted Section* is a main-story quest in Act 3, after the player has started to doubt (for example after *The Torn Page* (see [[Planned quests]]) shows a page is missing from the oldest book, and the cut edge leads here). Nobody gives it: the player decides to go in.

1. **Find a way in.** The key never leaves the Keepers. Options to plan: take the key from [[Brother Tomas]]'s ring while he dozes in the great hall, climb in through the high window from the Emberhall roof, or squeeze past the grille at night when the lamps are low.
2. **Sneak in at night.** Keepers walk the library at night. Stay out of their lanterns' light (Sneaking).
3. **Read what's hidden.** Each case holds one secret (below). Reading them is the quest's checklist, in any order.
4. **Get out unseen.** If a Keeper catches you, you're marched out and the grille gets a second lock, and you try again another night.

## What's inside

- **The oldest book, whole.** The account of the Circle's working, written before the [[Sundering War]]: the missing page from the public copy. It proves the Emberfall wasn't the land's fire waking on its own (see [[The Emberfall (disaster)]]).
- **The Oath of the Flame, in the old tongue.** The real words, with a translation in the margin. They aren't a promise. They're a binding spell (see [[Oath of the Flame]]).
- **The Ceremony books.** The true records of every [[Radiance Ceremony]]: how much ember each champion gave, and where it went. "To the High Keeper."
- **The Mages' Guild's records.** Everything the Guild knew about [[The Emberheart]], taken after the Sundering War.
- **A letter from Wick.** Written to Ivy during the war, never delivered. He was trying to stop the Ceremony, not the shrines.

## Why Lisette lies

Lisette isn't one of the corrupt [[The Undying|Keepers]]. She has never been inside either. Every excuse is one Keeper Aldric gave her, and over the years she's started to notice they don't add up. If the player finds her after the quest, she asks, quietly, what was in there.
