**The Rebuilding** is the two hundred years after [[The Emberfall (disaster)|the Emberfall]], when the land was taken back from the wild ember. People also call it the age of heroes.

- **The great shrines (0 to 10):** each member of [[The Ember Circle]] raised a great shrine in their own region to pin the torrent down (see [[The shrines]]).
- **The first Emberborn (about 30):** the shrines began catching the wild ember and giving it bodies. The first [[Emberborn]], Halric the Dawnbringer among them, fought the burning beasts and stabilized the land around each shrine.
- **The Emberguard (about 50):** the Circle founded [[The Emberguard]] to keep the shrines lit and train the new Emberborn.
- **The cities:** survivors gathered where the land was warm and safe, around the great shrines, and each shrine became the heart of a city. [[Emberhold]] grew up around [[The Heart Shrine]].

By about year 200 the land was under control again. That's also when the first Emberborn began to fade ([[The Fading]]), and the first [[Radiance Ceremony]] was held.

The Rebuilding is the one part of history where the official story and the truth mostly agree: the Circle really were heroes then.
